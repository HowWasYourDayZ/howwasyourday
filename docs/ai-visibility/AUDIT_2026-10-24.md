## 1. Current findings

### Can crawlers and AI assistants read LeanOn? Yes, verified

- **Crawler access:** every crawler got HTTP 200 on `/`, `/for-women`, `/answers` and `/marriage-problems-india`. That covers browser, Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, GPTBot, ChatGPT-User and PerplexityBot.
- **`robots.txt`:** all of these are allowed. Only private paths are blocked (`/api/`, `/session/`, `/dashboard/`, `/wallet/`, `/admin/`, `/profile/`, `/sessions/`, `/auth`). AI bots are also blocked from the 4 recruitment pages, which is intended.
- **Sitemaps:** `sitemap.xml` has 461 URLs. None is noindex or a redirect source, and none points at a missing route.
- **Page checks (69 URLs):**

### Product barriers found (code). Not fixed: they need your decision

1. **Australia cannot sign in.**
   - `app/auth/page.tsx` has no +61 country.
   - `lib/geo-pricing.ts` has no AU currency.
   - Yet `/australia`, `/australia-talk-to-someone`, `/australia-nri-support` and `/nri-loneliness-australia` are in the sitemap.
   - I removed Australia from the `/talk` FAQ and its structured data. The four Australia pages still lead to a dead end.
2. **The wallet is India-priced for overseas users.**
   - `app/wallet/page.tsx` shows "₹160 for 15 min" style labels and preselects ₹200 when coming back from `/talk`.
   - Overseas sessions cost about ₹840+ (US$10), so NRIs land on a top-up that is too small.
   - Checkout is INR-only Razorpay, so whether international cards work depends on the account.
3. **When nobody is online, `/talk` offers only "Try again" or "See all listeners".**
   - There is no "notify me when someone is free" and no leave-a-message option.
   - `/talk-to-someone-right-now` suggests such an option exists.
4. **There is no age check at sign-in.**
   - There is now a written 18+ line at sign-in, but no confirmation step and no date of birth.
   - Only listeners are age-checked.
5. **`WhatsAppSupport.tsx` hardcodes a phone number.** Confirm it is not the owner's personal or admin number; CLAUDE.md says admin phone numbers live only in env vars.

- **Questions:** `docs/ai-visibility/test-questions.csv` (20 questions, 4 per topic group).
- **Results:** record each in `docs/ai-visibility/tracking-template.csv`.
- **How to run it:**
  - Use **one new ChatGPT Temporary Chat per question**, with search on. Temporary Chats don't use memory or your history.
  - Don't ask follow-up questions, and don't mention LeanOn.
  - Repeat the same 20 in Perplexity, Google (AI Overview) and Copilot.
