# How Was Your Day: 50 SEO / AEO / GEO search-intent opportunities (24 October 2026)

> Research and implementation brief. **Not a list of the most searched ChatGPT prompts.** No public first-party ChatGPT prompt-frequency data or How Was Your Day Search Console/GA4 export was available for this work. Rank (P1/P2) represents *qualitative relevance and conversion-fit judgment*, **not** measured search volume, keyword difficulty, impressions, traffic, or revenue.

## Goal and constraints

Capture adults in India and the Indian diaspora seeking **non-clinical one-to-one conversations with a screened human peer listener**, primarily about relationships, marital loneliness, women's emotional load, stress, loneliness and needing to vent. Do not represent How Was Your Day as mental-health treatment, psychiatric care, relationship counselling, domestic-abuse assistance, or a crisis line. No promise of anonymity, instant availability, match success, credentials not evidenced, outcomes, or low prices without clear qualifications.

## How these 50 were chosen

1. Thematic fit with How Was Your Day's actual seeker journey (rather than broad informational traffic or listener hiring intent).
2. Typical natural-language ways a person might describe a need in ChatGPT or Google; no claim these are verified exact frequent prompts.
3. India's relationship and family context and existing How Was Your Day pages (URL audit of branch geo/ai-search-factuality-2026-10-17-v2).
4. Prioritize pages closest to high-intent, safe conversions; map synonyms to one canonical page, not 50 doorway pages.

**To rank by actual Google opportunity:** Export Google Search Console Queries + Pages for last 3 months, filter India, compare impressions/clicks/CTR/rank; exclude branded and listener-recruitment queries; add Google Keyword Planner / third-party volume estimates where licensed. **For ChatGPT:** inspect GA4 referral sessions from chatgpt.com and source UTMs, use Bing Webmaster Tools and periodic reproducible manual prompt audits. Prompts here are hypotheses until validated; generative answers vary by user/context.

## Technical and editorial implementation

- Add an original evergreen **decision guide** at `/relationship-emotional-support-guide-india`: plain-language answers to common uncertainties about relationship difficulties, family pressure and when to choose a listener vs a qualified professional, with links to canonical topic pages. Guide must earn its place; avoid 50 low-value near-duplicate pages.
- Link the guide prominently from `/support`, add a sitemap entry, and provide concise topic-discovery context in `/llms.txt`.
- Existing indexable, canonical pages remain primary landing pages above. Avoid competing duplicates: do not create 50 pages or add artificial FAQ rich-result claims. Structured data, where present, should exactly match visible content.
- Preserve clear safety and trust facts: users 18+, phone OTP for sign-in, listener sees display name not phone, messages retained and subject to safety review, no guarantee of immediate online listener, free intro once per account up to 5 minutes, paid rate varies by mode/listener, not therapy or a crisis service.
- Fix verifiably misleading global copy on `/support` claiming listeners are always online 24/7 and hiding paid-session qualifications.
